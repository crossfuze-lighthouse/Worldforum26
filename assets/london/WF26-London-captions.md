## Maturity Index
**Image:** WF26-London-05-maturity-index.png
**Slot:** Post 5 · Readiness benchmark
**Date:** Publish 9 October

The enterprise average AI maturity score is 51. Where do you stand?

ServiceNow's Enterprise AI Maturity Index 2026 surveyed 4,500 executives across 19 countries and 12 industries. The average score rose to 51, up from 35 the year before, and only one in five organisations reached the Pacesetter band. Those that did report an average ROI of 160% on their AI investments.

The Crossfuze AI Readiness Benchmark gives you your own number. It scores your estate across data and knowledge quality, workflow maturity, governance and controls, operating ownership, and readiness across six business functions.

It runs in your browser. Five minutes, no sign-up, and the result is yours to export.

Run it before World Forum London on 20 October, then bring your score to the Crossfuze stand and we will walk through what to fix first.

Source: ServiceNow, Enterprise AI Maturity Index 2026.
