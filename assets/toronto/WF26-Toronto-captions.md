## Readiness Number
**Image:** WF26-Toronto-05-readiness-number.png
**Slot:** Post 5 · Readiness benchmark
**Date:** Publish 23 October

Put a number on your AI readiness.

One in five organizations are Pacesetters. They report 160% average ROI on AI.

ServiceNow's Enterprise AI Maturity Index 2026 surveyed 4,500 executives across 19 countries and 12 industries. Only one in five organizations reached the Pacesetter band, and those that did report an average ROI of 160% on their AI investments, projected to rise to 194% next year. The overall average score is 51.

The Crossfuze AI Readiness Benchmark gives you your own number. It scores your estate across data and knowledge quality, workflow maturity, governance and controls, operating ownership, and readiness across six business functions.

It runs in your browser. Five minutes, no sign-up, and the result is yours to export.

Run it before World Forum Toronto on 3 November, then bring your score to the Crossfuze booth and we will walk through what to fix first.
